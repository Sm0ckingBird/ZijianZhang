Teaching assistant for Columbia's introductory Operating Systems course, holding office hours and grading programming assignments covering processes, threads, synchronization, virtual memory, and file systems. This experience later contributed to [ezFS](/publication/2025-02-18-ezFS), a pedagogical Linux file system co-designed for the course and published at SIGCSE 2025.
